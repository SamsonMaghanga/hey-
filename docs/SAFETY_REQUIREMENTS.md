# Safety requirements
Synthetic demo data only. Ask users not to enter real personal or health information. No diagnosis, clinical promises, AI mental health chatbot, live triage/chat, bookings, actual notifications or third-party tracking.

Use predetermined non-diagnostic reflections and optional actions without promises of relief. Never infer risk from reflection text. Help is accessible independently of emotion. Explain that nobody monitors entries. For immediate danger, direct people to local emergency services or a nearby trusted person without inventing geographic phone numbers.

Each directory entry visibly says example/unverified and contains no live contact or booking action. Direct users to their institution's official website for verified contacts. Routine settings schedule nothing and request no notification permission.

All inputs stay in React memory. No logging, storage or transmission. Reset clears current state; refresh clears entries. This is not encrypted storage or protection against someone seeing an open screen. Real-data use requires separate consent, privacy, security and safeguarding review.

Legacy Vite screens remain for preservation only and are outside this prototype's approved scope.
