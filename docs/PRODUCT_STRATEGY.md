# Product strategy
Hey! explores whether a short check-in connected to an actionable coping step or relevant practical/human support makes reflection more useful. This is an unvalidated hypothesis, not a claim of clinical effectiveness.

Initial audience: university students aged 18–25, especially international students away from support networks. Prioritize autonomy, plain language, low effort and cultural variation. Never assume family is a safe contact.

Use synthetic-data usability sessions to learn whether students understand suggestions, can skip steps and can distinguish example services from real support. Ask about perceived usefulness without interpreting it as a health outcome. No tracking is installed. The first release is a demonstrable Care Loop, not a clinical service.
