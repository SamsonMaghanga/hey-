# Roadmap
1. Current: smallest synthetic-data Care Loop, seven original actions, mock directory, temporary reflection, routine preference and validation.
2. After review: student usability sessions, including international students; manual screen-reader/mobile checks; language and example-label comprehension review. No health data collection through this prototype.
3. With separate approval: investigate ownership of verified support information and whether any persistence is justified; define jurisdiction-specific help and privacy/security requirements.

Deferred: accounts, stored journals, real notifications, live contacts, chat and bookings. Deployment/publication requires explicit approval. No clinical effectiveness claim is supported.
